Main Flagship

Tutorial Haven

Tutorial Haven is a comprehensive EdTech platform helping Nigerian students prepare for JAMB, WAEC, NECO, and O'Level exams. With 950+ active students, ranked by ChatGPT as one of Nigeria's top 3 practice platforms, and AI-powered recommendations driving traffic. Features include past question drills, personalized learning, real-time tutoring, mock exams, subject notes, and the newly launched Tutorial Haven Arena—a gamified social learning subdomain with features like dueling quizzes, squad battles, leaderboards, daily missions, and more. Now running a full tutor directory and vetting pipeline — document check, video/voice intro, and CBT quiz — connecting verified tutors with students across Lagos for in-person and online tutoring.

LagSciPrep

LagSciPrep is a comprehensive Computer-Based Testing (CBT) platform developed specifically for science students at the University of Lagos — winner of Best Innovative Project at IED 2026. It features objective questions across biology, chemistry, physics, and more, with 901 registered users across 45 departments, 4,300+ CBT sessions run, and a growing 17,500+ question bank. The platform offers customizable mock exams, real-time progress tracking, and detailed result analytics to help students prepare effectively.

Squad Hackathon 3.0 — My First Hackathon

From 1,600 applicants to the shortlisted 250. Here is what happened.

🚀 Squad Hackathon 3.0 · May 2026 · Lagos, Nigeria

We Built OjaPass in Under Two Weeks

This was my first hackathon — and my entire team's first. Out of over 1,600 applications, Team SmartFlow was shortlisted to compete in person in Lagos. We spent sleepless nights building OjaPass, a full economic identity platform designed to bring Nigeria's 90 million informal workers into the formal financial system.

Six Squad API integrations. A Bayesian credit scoring engine. A gig marketplace with escrow. Digital Ajo savings. A loan engine with real amortisation logic. AI-powered job matching via Gemini. All of it — in under two weeks.

We ranked 77th overall. We didn't make the semifinals. But we walked away with something worth more than a podium — the ignition. The proof that we can build real things that solve real problems for real people.

About Me

I'm the founder and developer of Tutorial Haven, an EdTech platform dedicated to helping students prepare effectively for JAMB, WAEC, NECO, and other O’Level exams. I combine technical expertise with a deep commitment to education to create tools that truly support student success.

My mission is to make quality exam preparation accessible to every learner through innovative features like AI-powered tutoring, past question practice, and personalized learning paths—empowering students to achieve their goals with confidence.
