The Challenge — Intelligent Money for Everyone

The theme was clear: financial technology should be intelligent, accessible, and inclusive. The remittance corridor between the UK/US and Nigeria/Ghana is one of the busiest in the world — and one of the most expensive. Traditional services charge high fees, offer poor exchange rates, and provide limited control over how transactions work.

We asked ourselves: what if we gave users more control? What if we used AI to optimize their transactions? What if we built something that actually solved the problems people face when sending money home?

That question led to Bridgr.

What We Built — Bridgr

In under 24 hours, including an overnight build sprint at NITHUB, we designed, built, and shipped a fully functional Django web app. Here's what Bridgr does:

  • 💸
    Fee Optimization Engine — users choose whether sender pays, recipient pays, or both split the transaction fee. This is a feature not offered by Wise, LemFi, or any major competitor. It gives users full control over cost distribution.
  • 🛡️
    Fair Fee Shield — locks the exchange rate at quote time, protecting users from naira volatility between confirmation and processing. No surprise deductions when the transfer completes.
  • 🏫
    Direct Institution Payments — funds routed directly to verified schools, hospitals, and businesses without passing through a personal account. Reduces friction and increases trust for institutional transfers.
  • 🤖
    AI Fee Recommendation — Gemini API analyzes user transfer history and recipient type to suggest the optimal fee distribution before the user chooses. Smart suggestions, not random choices.
  • 💱
    BMONI API Integration — live exchange rates, transfer initiation, account verification, and webhook-based status updates all powered by BMONI's sandbox. Real fintech, not mock data.
  • 🏪
    Vendor Marketplace — marketplace for verified informal exchange agents, connecting users with trusted local options for cash transactions.

The Grind — 24 Hours at NITHUB

We accepted the hackathon on July 28. Arrived at the Innovation Fair venue on July 29 at 3PM for the launch. By 5PM, we'd moved to NITHUB and started building.

We built through the night. No sleep. Just code, coffee, and the occasional break to stretch. The NITHUB space became our world — laptops open, screens glowing, ideas flowing.

My role was full-stack development with focus on backend architecture and BMONI integration. I built the Django REST Framework backend, integrated BMONI's sandbox API for live rates and transfers, and implemented the AI layer using Gemini API for fee recommendations.

"The best hackathons aren't about the prize — they're about what you prove to yourself you can build."

We submitted within the 3-5PM window on July 30. The submission wasn't just code — it was a fully functional web app that actually worked. Real API calls. Real transfers (in sandbox). Real user flows.

On July 31, we pitched. We were selected as one of the top 7 teams to present to the judging panel. Standing there, showing what we'd built in 24 hours, I felt something I'd felt before — the proof that we can build real things that solve real problems.

What We Learned

Lesson 01
Real APIs force you to think about edge cases.

When you're working with mock data, everything works perfectly. When you're working with real fintech APIs, you have to handle rate limits, network failures, webhook delays, and authentication tokens that expire. Building with BMONI's sandbox taught us to think about production realities, not just happy paths.

Lesson 02
Fee distribution is a competitive advantage.

We discovered that none of the major remittance services offer users the choice to decide who pays fees. Sender pays, recipient pays, or split — this simple feature gives users control they don't have elsewhere. Sometimes the best innovations aren't complex tech — they're giving users options nobody else thought to offer.

Lesson 03
AI should enhance, not replace, user choice.

Our AI fee recommendation doesn't make the decision for the user — it suggests the optimal choice based on their history and recipient type. The user still decides. AI as advisor, not dictator. That's how AI should work in financial products.

Lesson 04
Overnight builds test more than code — they test team dynamics.

Building through the night reveals how a team handles stress, fatigue, and pressure. Zeroday worked well together — clear communication, mutual support, shared vision. That's what got us through the 24-hour sprint and into the top 7 finalists.
